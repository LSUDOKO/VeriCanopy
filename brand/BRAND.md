## Mark
Three dark canopy facets (an isometric crown) over a lime check-chevron: the forest, and the verification under it. The small lime plus marks additionality. Minimum size 20 px. Clear space = height of the plus on every side. Never rotate, recolour outside the palette, or add drop shadows.

## Type
Geist (Google Fonts) for everything; Geist Mono for hashes, numbers and code.
Headlines weight 600, tracking −0.03em, line-height 1.02. Body 400 at 17 px / 1.5. Labels 500 at 13 px, tracking 0.

## Spacing and shape
8 px grid. Section padding 96–128 px desktop, 64 px mobile. Card radius 20 px, buttons 980 px pill, inputs 12 px, 1 px hairline borders, no heavy shadows (`0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.06)` max on cards, `0 20px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.18)` only on floating media).

## Voice
Short declarative sentences. Lead with the proof: "Scored from satellite pixels. Proven on-chain. Signed for decades." Never "demo", never hype; always name the dataset, the chain and the testnet.
